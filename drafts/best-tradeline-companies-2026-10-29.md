# Best Tradeline Companies: What to Compare and What to Ask Before You Choose

**Short version:** We're not going to hand you a ranked list. We sell tradelines, so any ranking from us would be self-serving, and rankings on affiliate sites often follow commissions. Instead, here's a comparison table and a scorecard you can use to judge any company, including ours.

## "Best" depends on what you need

| If you are... | "Best" probably means... |
|---|---|
| A buyer with a closing or application date | A clear reporting date and order-by date, shown before you pay, and a written guarantee |
| Someone burned by another company | Transparency, plain answers, and a person you can call |
| A loan officer, agent, or credit professional | Predictable timing, communication you can pass to a client, and honest guidance on fit |
| A business buying in volume | Broker or wholesale terms (ours offer discounts of up to 20%; confirm current terms) |
| A cardholder who wants to supply | Clear supplier requirements, payment terms, and protections |

## Transparent practices vs. common ones

You'll see these two patterns across the industry. Neither describes any single company.

| | Transparent practice | Common practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Line details** | Bank, limit, age, reporting date, order-by date shown before payment | "Contact us for details" or details revealed after payment |
| **Guarantee** | Written, with terms and conditions you can read | Verbal assurance or marketing language |
| **Guarantee scope** | That the tradeline will post, subject to its terms | Implied or stated score gains |
| **Urgency** | Tied to a real order-by date | Countdown timers, "only 2 left" |
| **Fit** | Tells you when a tradeline won't help | Every visitor is a good fit |
| **Contact** | Named staff and a physical office | Form or chat only |
| **Identity** | Authorized user tradelines only | Mentions of CPNs or "new credit identity" |

## A 10-point scorecard

Give each company one point per "yes." Anything under 7 deserves more questions.

1. Bank, limit, and account age visible before payment?
2. Reporting date and order-by date visible before payment?
3. Guarantee available in writing?
4. Guarantee limited to posting, with no score or approval promises?
5. Explains statement date vs. reporting date vs. bureau update?
6. Says who may not benefit?
7. Named people you can contact?
8. A physical address and phone number?
9. Clear pricing and extension pricing?
10. No CPN or "new identity" language anywhere?

## Why we don't publish a ranked list

- **We have a conflict of interest.** We'd put ourselves first, and you'd be right not to trust that.
- **"Best" shifts with your deadline.** The right line depends on your file and your reporting dates.
- **Inventory and terms change.** A list written today may be wrong next month.

Where we can help is saying how we'd score ourselves. We show the bank, limit, account age, reporting date, and order-by date before payment. Our written guarantee covers posting, with conditions, and does not cover score changes or approvals. If a tradeline isn't likely to help your file, we'll say so before you buy. Check each of these against our current documents and judge for yourself.

## Before you choose anyone

1. Decide whether a tradeline fits your file. Recent negative items often outweigh any added account. See [Authorized User Tradelines: How It Works and Who It May Not Help](/authorized-user-tradelines).
2. Ask the eight questions in [Tradeline Companies: What to Compare and What to Ask](/tradeline-companies).
3. Know your deadline. Count back from your closing or application date, and leave time for reporting and bureau updates.
4. Read the guarantee. All of it.
